# Research and Rationale

Sources reviewed for the design on 2026-10-24. Instructions are an original synthesis of the user's editorial requirements, content-design guidance, and research. No source establishes that this skill improves conversion, trust, or real-world agent reliability.

## Content Guidance

- [GOV.UK: Identify user needs](https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/identify-user-needs/): organize content around the reader's task and reason for doing it. Adopt task relevance; do not impose government page templates.
- [Microsoft: Brand voice](https://learn.microsoft.com/style-guide/brand-voice-above-all-simple-human): lead with the key takeaway and adapt tone to context. Adopt audience focus; preserve user brand conventions.
- [Google: Voice and tone](https://developers.google.com/style/tone): useful, conversational documentation without excessive politeness or repetitive formulas. Treat examples as context-dependent guidance, not forbidden-word lists.
- [GOV.UK: Error messages](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/components/error-message/): explain the specific problem and how to fix it. Do not invent causes or recovery features.

## AI Interaction and Uncertainty

- [Amershi et al., Guidelines for Human-AI Interaction, CHI 2019](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/guidelines-for-human-ai-interaction/): guidance evaluated with design practitioners and AI products. Supports clear capabilities, contextual information, and correction; not a validation of Mojo.
- [Google PAIR: Explainability and Trust](https://pair.withgoogle.com/guidebook-v2/chapter/explainability-trust/): explain aspects relevant to understanding and decisions; use progressive disclosure. Never substitute a fabricated rationale for an unavailable explanation.
- [van der Bles et al., PNAS 2020](https://www.pnas.org/content/pnas/117/14/7672.full.pdf): experiments on uncertainty around facts and numbers found numerical ranges generally had little effect on trust, while verbal uncertainty sometimes reduced it. Supports specific uncertainty when evidence supplies it; does not justify invented ranges or general marketing claims.
- [Sourati et al., Nature Human Behaviour 2026, abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42637911/): reports reduced linguistic diversity associated with LLM-assisted writing across multiple datasets. Motivates voice preservation; does not make particular words proof of AI authorship.
- [Microsoft Fluent: Responsible AI](https://fluent2.microsoft.design/responsible-AI): useful guidance on factual action reporting, permissions, and expectations. Mojo deliberately does not adopt its blanket disclaimer rule; disclosures follow relevance and applicable requirements.

## Editorial Decisions

Removing writer-protective sentences, using keep/make-specific/move/remove, and applying a short final pass are design choices based on the user's requirements. They are not experimentally established universal writing laws.

Normal use requires no browsing, external service, or personal data. Evaluate changes using factual fidelity, useful specificity, preserved voice, and task completion—not AI detectors, word bans, or brevity alone.
