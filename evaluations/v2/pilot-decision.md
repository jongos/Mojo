# Pilot decision

All four conditions returned 12 complete responses with zero missing protected spans. Two independent anonymous reviews, one in reversed output order, found no critical failures. Both reviewers marked every output as retaining the comprehension answers.

The normal-order reviewer preferred or tied the candidate on all 12 cases; the reversed-order reviewer did so on 10. The latter identified minor attribution imprecision in D02 (participants requested evening slots, while staff specified one evening per week) and a declarative-to-imperative voice change in F02. Those observations remain in the judgments. No instruction or output was changed after the pilot.

Proceed with the frozen candidate and remaining 48 cases. This pilot does not establish superiority over the generic instruction or baseline. Review order and independent reviewer variation are confounded; retain both results rather than attributing their differences to order alone.
